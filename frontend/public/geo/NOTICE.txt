The country lookup files contain data from DB-IP IP to Country Lite, November 2026.
IP Geolocation by DB-IP (https://db-ip.com/), CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The original CSV was converted to fixed-width binary range records and ZZ entries were removed.

The simplified world GeoJSON is derived from Natural Earth Admin 0 Countries, 1:110m,
version 5.1.2 (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/). Natural Earth is public domain.
NORTH retains the country identifiers and label positions and rounds geometry coordinates.
